Answering questions is a commodity. We're not selling answers.

The economics work in our favour

Since July 2025 Meta charges per template message — but replies inside the 24-hour window the learner opens are free. Every engagement loop is designed so the learner messages first: marginal session cost is LLM tokens in the cents, controlled by a cheap intent-router model.

A legally clean content moat

Our corpus is DBE-published material — CAPS statements, ATPs, NSC past papers with marking memos, all public. No textbook licences to buy, no copyright exposure, and structured memo data that powers real marks, which embeddings-only competitors can't fake.

Roadmap: prove the loop, then widen the moat

P0 weeks 1–6

Prove the loop with a Grade 8 Mathematics pilot: CAPS-grounded Socratic help plus practice and mastery tracking that bring learners back tomorrow. The POC de-risks this phase today.

P1 months 2–4

Become a tutor, not a chatbot: timed quizzes marked vs memos, PDF report cards, the mastery graph driving daily revision missions, shared-phone profiles.

P2 months 4–8

Widen the moat: code-switched voice, isiZulu/Sepedi replies, Study Squads, airtime micro-rewards, the data-free Moya channel, weekly parent digest.

P3 months 8+

Distribution & revenue: USSD lookup, teacher dashboards, CSR / B-BBEE sponsorship packages, school licensing.
