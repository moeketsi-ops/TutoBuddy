Explicitly not buying

  • BSP platform fees (Twilio, Infobip, MessageBird) — we go direct to Meta's Cloud API, which is free.
  • A dedicated vector database (Pinecone, Weaviate) — pgvector inside our existing Postgres covers retrieval until scale actually hurts.
  • GPUs or model fine-tuning — prompted frontier models + curriculum RAG beat a fine-tune on accuracy per rand; revisit only if measured vernacular performance demands it (P2+).
  • Textbook licences — our corpus is free, public DBE material (CAPS, ATPs, NSC papers with memos). That is a feature, not a gap.
  • Native app development — the product lives in WhatsApp; that's the thesis.
