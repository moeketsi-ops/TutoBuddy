How It Works

Meet Katlego — the Achieva AI tutor who lives where your learner already is.

No app to download, no login to remember. Katlego is a WhatsApp contact — as familiar as messaging a friend, available whenever a question comes up.

Just WhatsApp

If a learner already knows how to send a voice note, they already know how to use TutoBuddy.

Katlego is added as a WhatsApp contact — the same app already open on the phone for schoolwork groups and family chats. A learner types a question, sends a photo of a textbook page, or asks Katlego to walk through a topic from scratch.

WhatsApp social/data bundles run ~R29–R99/month depending on network, and many networks zero-rate WhatsApp text entirely — so reaching Katlego doesn't compete with a family's data budget the way a dedicated app would.

A Sample Exchange

Katlego doesn't just hand over the answer — it checks for understanding first.

Katlego · Achieva AI Tutor
Hi Katlego, I don't get how to factorise x² + 5x + 6 😩
No stress, let's break it down together. We need two numbers that multiply to 6 and add to 5. Can you think of a pair?
2 and 3?
Exactly right! So x² + 5x + 6 becomes (x + 2)(x + 3). Want to try one on your own now, or see one more worked example first?
A Session, Step By Step

Four simple beats, repeated for every topic.

1 · Ask
Learner asks

A question, a photo of a textbook page, or "explain this topic from scratch."

2 · Guide
Katlego guides

Socratic-style prompts and worked examples — not just the final answer.

3 · Check
Understanding is checked

A quick practice problem updates that topic's mastery score.

4 · Adapt
The next step adapts

Remediate, practice, challenge, or advance — based on that score, not a fixed schedule.

What's Covered

Grade 8–12, starting with Mathematics.

TutoBuddy launches CAPS-aligned to Mathematics for Grades 8–12, with one to two additional core subjects following at MVP. Lower grades are planned within 12 months as the curriculum knowledge graph expands.

Every topic is mapped to the same mastery-tracking engine, so a learner's progress is consistent whether they're revising for a Friday quiz or catching up on a topic from earlier in the year.

Safe by design.

Katlego is a learning tool, not an open-ended chatbot. Conversations stay on-curriculum, consent and data-retention flows are POPIA-first, and the mastery engine gives parents and schools a transparent way to see progress rather than a black box.

On-curriculumKatlego is scoped to CAPS-aligned subjects and topics, not general-purpose chat.
